Projects

Simulation of Single-Stage Boost Converter Integrated with Buck Converter for EV Applications

Designed a single-stage buck-boost DC-DC converter that can both increase and decrease voltage using an inductor, MOSFET switch, and diode to transfer energy efficiently. Useful in systems where input voltage varies; the simulation analyzes performance under different conditions and ensures stable voltage for devices like batteries and solar systems.

Design and Fabrication of Solar Tree for Optimal Mobile Charging Application

Designed a mini solar tree that maximizes solar energy by adjusting panel angles to capture sunlight more efficiently throughout the day. Improves power output for reliable mobile charging; the system is compact, space-saving, ideal for urban use, and ensures optimal charging even during low-sunlight conditions.
